# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in the EverSmooth Optimized project and its community.

We value constructive collaboration, mutual respect, and meaningful contributions that help improve the project.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and professional.
- Treat everyone fairly, regardless of experience or background.
- Communicate constructively and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing opinions, perspectives, and approaches.
- Provide helpful and relevant feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its improvement.
- Respect the work, licenses, and contributions of others.
- Help maintain a friendly and collaborative environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech, insults, or offensive comments.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated inflammatory behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Spamming issues, pull requests, discussions, or community channels.
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or falsely claiming ownership of others' work.
- Deliberately misleading contributors or community members.
- Any other behavior considered inappropriate or harmful to the community.

## Contribution Guidelines

When participating in this project:

- Submit relevant and well-described issues.
- Provide clear explanations when reporting bugs or suggesting features.
- Ensure pull requests are focused and properly documented.
- Avoid unnecessary discussions or unrelated changes.
- Respect maintainers' decisions and project guidelines.
- Do not submit content that violates third-party licenses or intellectual property rights.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- Removing inappropriate comments or contributions.
- Issuing warnings.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation in project spaces.

The severity of the response will depend on the nature and circumstances of the violation.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the official project contact channels.

Please include relevant details so the situation can be reviewed appropriately.

All reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed as fairly as possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Official community platforms.
- Other spaces where individuals represent or participate in EverSmooth Optimized.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in EverSmooth Optimized, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, welcoming, and productive community.
